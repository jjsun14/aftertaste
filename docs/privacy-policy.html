Aftertaste Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 24, 2026

Aftertaste ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal food journal app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is stored.

Data We Collect

  • Account information — Email address and password (for authentication).
  • Profile information — Display name and username you choose to set.
  • Memories & visits — Restaurant names, ratings, notes, occasion tags, and other details you enter when logging a meal.
  • Photos — Images you attach to memories, uploaded to our cloud storage.
  • Location — Your device location is used only to find nearby restaurants. We do not track or store your location history.
  • Friends — Usernames of friends you connect with in the app.
  • Push notification tokens — Device tokens for delivering notifications, if you opt in.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide app functionality: saving your memories, showing nearby restaurants, connecting with friends, and sending notifications you opted into. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — Hosts our database, authentication, and photo storage. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Foursquare — Provides restaurant search results. Your search queries and approximate location are sent to Foursquare to return results. Foursquare Privacy Policy
  • Mapbox — Provides maps and location geocoding. Mapbox Privacy Policy

Data Storage & Security

All data is stored in Supabase with row-level security enabled, meaning users can only access their own data. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. All network communication uses HTTPS encryption.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen in the app. This permanently removes all your data including memories, photos, friend connections, and your account. This action cannot be undone.

Children

Aftertaste is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
